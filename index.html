Trend Estilistas

Envisioning new trends from the capitals of the worlds offered to our local community at an affordable price, yet never lowering the quality. Everyone deserves to be able to have beautiful hair. Trend Estilistas began in Albir in 2017, driven by Jose Antonio's passion for international city-quality hair services. Now expanded to La Nucia, redefining luxury, adding a Japanese Head Spa and creating a 360° salon for all your needs.
